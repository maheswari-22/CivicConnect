# CivicConnect colour system (60 · 30 · 10)

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| 60% — page / cards | Champagne Beige + tints | #F2E5C6 · page #F8F2E3 · card #FFFBF2 · section #F3EAD3 |
| 30% — structure | Burgundy / Dark Maroon / Deep Wine | #75162D · #560B18 · #3B010B |
| 10% — accent | Sand Gold (+ deeper tones for use on beige) | #F2D9A0 · fill #E3C078 · line #D9B15A · text #94681F |

Sand Gold is used as-is on wine surfaces. On beige it is too pale, so the deeper tones above (derived from it) are used for fills, lines and text.
Status colours (green success, red error, teal for "in progress") are kept distinct from burgundy. Photos, logos and character art are untouched.
